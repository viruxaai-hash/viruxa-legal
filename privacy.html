Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 July 2026  ·  App: Viruxa (com.viruxa.app)

The short version. Viruxa scans your phone on the device itself. Your files, messages and app contents are analysed locally and are never uploaded to us. You can use the whole app without an account. We do not sell or share your personal data, and you can delete your data from inside the app at any time.

This policy explains what information Viruxa ("we", "us", "the app") collects, why, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the Viruxa mobile app and its backend service.

1. Our core principle: on-device analysis

Viruxa is a security app, so it inspects apps, files, messages, links and network activity to protect you. Wherever possible this analysis happens entirely on your device. The actual content of your files and messages does not leave your phone. When a reputation check against threat databases is needed, only anonymous indicators — such as a file's cryptographic hash (SHA-256) or a URL you asked us to check — are sent, never the file or message itself.

2. Information we collect

a. Account information (optional)

An account is optional and only used to carry your subscription and breach-monitoring settings to a new device. If you create one, we store:

  • Your email address (or the email from your Google sign-in), used to identify your account.
  • A display name, if you choose to add one.
  • Your password is stored only as a salted hash — we never store or see the plain password.

b. Subscription information

If you buy a subscription, the purchase is processed by Google Play Billing (and, for verification, RevenueCat). We store the resulting purchase token and subscription status so we can unlock premium features. We never receive or store your card or bank details — the app store handles payment.

c. Security & threat events

To show you your own scan history and protect you, we may store the results of scans (for example, that a risky app or file was found, an app name, a file hash, or a URL you checked). These are tied to a device identifier, not to your identity, unless you are signed in.

d. Device identifier

We generate a random device ID so a subscription can be bound to your device. It is not your phone number, IMEI, or advertising ID.

e. What we do NOT collect

  • We do not upload your contacts, photos, or documents.
  • We do not upload the content of your SMS messages — smishing detection runs on-device.
  • We do not collect your precise location or your web-browsing history.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or sell your data.

3. How we use information

  • To authenticate you and sync your subscription across your devices.
  • To detect malware, scams, phishing, and fraud, and to alert you.
  • To provide optional breach / dark-web monitoring for an email address you give us.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the security engine.

6. Data retention & your choices

  • Delete my data: In the app, go to Settings → Privacy & data → Delete my data to permanently remove your subscription record and this device's data from our servers.
  • Delete account: If you created an account, deleting it removes your email and profile from our servers.
  • Scan results stored for your history can be cleared from the Scan tab at any time.
  • We keep account and subscription data only as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service.

7. Security

All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

8. Children

Viruxa is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International users

Our servers may process and store data in regions where our providers operate. By using Viruxa you consent to this processing consistent with this policy.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above, and where appropriate, in the app.
