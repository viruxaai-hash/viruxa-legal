Delete your account & data

App: Vift AI (com.viruxa.app)  ·  Last updated: 2 August 2026

This page explains how to delete your Vift AI account and the data associated with it. You can do it yourself inside the app in seconds, or request it by email — no reinstall required.

Option 1 — Delete it yourself in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Vift AI on your phone.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Open Privacy & data.
  4. Tap Delete my data and confirm.

If you created an account (email or Google sign-in), also tap Sign out → Delete account to remove your account record. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email viruxaai@gmail.com from the email address on your Vift AI account, with the subject line "Delete my account". We will verify the request and delete your account and associated data within 7 days, and confirm by reply.

Note: your subscription purchase is managed by Google Play, not by Vift AI. To stop billing, also cancel the subscription in Google Play → Subscriptions; deleting your Vift AI account does not by itself cancel a Google Play subscription.
